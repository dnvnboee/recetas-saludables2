Chicken and Avocado Salad

Preparation time: 15 to 20 minutes

Number of servings: 2

Approximate cost: $65.00 MXN to $85.00 MXN

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
1 Cooked and shredded chicken breast 150 grams
1 Ripe avocado diced 150 grams
2 Cups of chopped lettuce or spinach 2 Cups
1 Chopped tomato 1 Tomato
Juice of 1 lemon, salt, pepper, and olive oil To taste
For the chicken: 1 chicken breast, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and a drizzle of olive oil. To taste

Instructions

  1. Place the lettuce or spinach in a large bowl as a base
  2. Add the chicken, avocado, and tomato.
  3. Season with lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper to taste
  4. Mix gently to avoid mashing the avocado and serve immediately
  5. For the chicken: Season the breast with salt, black pepper, and garlic powder; cook in a skillet with a drizzle of olive oil until browned to your liking

Nutritional value (per serving)

Nutrient Amount
Energy 350 kcal
Protein 32g
Fat 18g
Carbohydrates 12g
Fiber 5g
Sodium 350 mg

Zucchini and Tomato Spaghetti

Preparation time: 15 minutes

Number of servings: 2

Approximate cost: $25.00 MXN to $40.00 MXN

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
10 Tomatoes halved 10
2 Medium zucchinis 2
1 Minced garlic clove 1
2 Tablespoons of olive oil 2
Salt and oregano To taste
Spaghetti 1-2 packs
Ground beef 30-40 pesos worth

Instructions

  1. Wash the zucchinis and cut them into long strips (spaghetti style) using a grater or spiralizer.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and sauté the minced garlic for one minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook for 3 minutes until soft.
  4. Bring a pot of water to a boil for the spaghetti.
  5. Once boiling, add the spaghetti and drain when cooked.
  6. Add the zucchini spaghetti, salt, and oregano.
  7. Sauté everything for 3 to 4 minutes until the zucchini is tender yet crisp, then remove from heat.
  8. Cook the ground beef for 5-10 minutes and add it to the spaghetti.

Nutritional value (per serving)

Nutrient Amount
Energy 140 kcal
Protein 3g
Fat 10g
Carbohydrates 12g
Fiber 4g
Sodium 200 mg

Skillet Broccoli Omelet

Preparation time: 15 minutes

Number of servings: 1 to 2

Approximate cost: $20.00 MXN to $30.00 MXN

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
1 Cup of broccoli cut into small florets 1 Cup
2 Eggs 2
1 Teaspoon of olive oil 1
Salt, pepper, and shredded cheese To taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the broccoli in salted water for 5 minutes until tender; drain well.
  2. Beat the eggs in a bowl with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat the oil in a small skillet and add the cooked broccoli.
  4. Pour the beaten eggs over the broccoli.
  5. Cook over low heat covered until the eggs set, sprinkle cheese on top, and brown to taste before folding or serving.

Nutritional value (per serving)

Nutrient Amount
Energy 210 kcal
Protein 14g
Fat 14g
Carbohydrates 6g
Fiber 2.5g
Sodium 320 mg

Salmon and Cucumber Rolls

Preparation time: 15 minutes

Number of servings: 2 to 3

Approximate cost: $80.00 MXN to $110.00 MXN

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
1 Large cucumber 1
150 g of smoked salmon strips 150 g
3 Tablespoons of light cream cheese (Philadelphia low fat) 3 Tablespoons
Fresh chopped dill or a squeeze of lemon To taste

Instructions

  1. Wash the cucumber and slice it into thin, long strips using a mandoline or peeler.
  2. Pat the cucumber strips dry with a paper towel.
  3. Spread a thin layer of cream cheese over each cucumber slice.
  4. Place a small strip of smoked salmon on top of the cream cheese.
  5. Roll carefully and garnish with dill or a few drops of lemon juice.

Nutritional value (per serving)

Nutrient Amount
Energy 230 kcal
Protein 16g
Fat 15g
Carbohydrates 7g
Fiber 1g
Sodium 650 mg

Chickpea Salad

Preparation time: 15 minutes

Number of servings: 3

Approximate cost: $35.00 MXN to $50.00 MXN

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
1 Cup of cooked chickpeas 1 Cup
Chopped lettuce, 1 cucumber, and 1 red bell pepper chopped 1 of each
1/2 Avocado sliced 1/2
Olive oil, salt, pepper, cumin, and paprika To taste
Lemon juice and a touch of mustard To taste
Grilled chicken breast Half breast

Instructions

  1. Season the chickpeas with salt, pepper, cumin, and paprika.
  2. Sauté them in a pan with a bit of olive oil until crispy.
  3. Mix the lettuce, cucumber, bell pepper, and avocado in a bowl.
  4. Add the warm chickpeas and drizzle with the lemon and mustard dressing.
  5. Cut the chicken breast into pieces and season with salt, pepper, and a bit of paprika.
  6. Cook the chicken pieces in a skillet to your preferred doneness.
  7. Finally, add it along with the rest of the ingredients.

Nutritional value (per serving)

Nutrient Amount
Energy 280 kcal
Protein 9g
Fat 14g
Carbohydrates 32g
Fiber 8g
Sodium 250 mg
